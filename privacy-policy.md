# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Alexaa Devi YouTube Automation** ("the application", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when the application connects to YouTube through Google OAuth 2.0.

This application is a private automation tool used by its owner to publish video content to the owner's YouTube channel.

## 1. Information we access

When you authorize the application with Google, the application may receive information and permissions provided through Google's OAuth authorization process, including:

- Google account authorization information.
- YouTube channel information needed to identify the authorized channel.
- Permission to upload and manage videos through the YouTube Data API.
- OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.

The application does not request your Google password.

## 2. How we use information

Information obtained through Google/YouTube APIs is used only to operate the application's YouTube publishing functionality, including:

- Uploading videos and Shorts.
- Publishing or scheduling content as supported by the connected YouTube API workflow.
- Setting video metadata such as titles, descriptions, tags, and visibility.
- Maintaining the authorized connection so automated uploads can continue.

We do not use Google user data for advertising or targeted marketing.

## 3. Data sharing

We do not sell Google user data.

We do not rent Google user data.

We do not share Google user data with third parties except where necessary to provide the application's requested functionality or where required by law.

## 4. Data storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored only as necessary to operate the authorized automation workflow and are protected using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

No security system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

OAuth credentials are retained only while the YouTube connection is needed for the automation.

You may revoke the application's access from your Google Account at any time. You may also request deletion of application-held OAuth credentials and related data by contacting:

**aksbytedev@gmail.com**

When deletion is requested, applicable OAuth credentials and associated application data will be deleted, subject to any information that must be retained to comply with legal obligations.

## 6. Google's APIs

The application's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

You can manage or revoke third-party application access through your Google Account settings.

## 7. Children's privacy

This application is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's functionality or data handling changes. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the application's handling of data, contact:
